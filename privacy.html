Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit ranglovesyou.com, why it's collected, and the choices you have. We try to keep it short and plain-spoken.

1. Information We Collect

  • Analytics data. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they look at. Google automatically receives information such as your IP address (truncated for privacy), browser and device type, pages viewed, time spent, and the page that referred you. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this.
  • Booking / contact form submissions. If you fill out the booking form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide so we can reply. The form is sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards the message to our booking inbox.
  • Embedded media. Pages on this site embed content from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, and Apple Music. When those embeds load, the third-party platforms may set their own cookies and collect data per their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive that data.
  • YouTube Data API. The site uses YouTube's public Data API to show the latest videos and subscriber count. This is a server-to-server request and does not personally identify you, but Google logs the request.

2. How We Use Information

  • To understand which pages and content resonate with visitors.
  • To respond to booking and contact inquiries.
  • To improve the site's design and content.

We don't sell your personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

The following third parties may receive data when you use this site:

  • Google Analytics — see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • FormSubmit — see FormSubmit's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube / Google — when embeds load, see Google's policy linked above.
  • TikTok — see TikTok's Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram (Meta) — see Instagram's Privacy Policy.
  • Spotify — see Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Music — see Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by your browser. This site uses two main types:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) — set when you visit so we can count visits and see general usage patterns.
  • Third-party embed cookies — set by YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Spotify, and Apple Music when their players load.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Doing so will not break the core site, but some embedded content may not work fully.

5. Your Choices

  • Opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by enabling your browser's "Do Not Track" or tracking-prevention setting.
  • Disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • Request that we delete any contact-form submission you've sent us by emailing the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent change.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or to make a privacy request, email rangmusicbiz@gmail.com.
